Aircon Types

Split-Type Aircon

The most common type for homes and offices. Consists of an indoor unit mounted high on the wall and an outdoor compresso...

Energy-efficient, quiet, wide capacity range, easy to install Wall-mounted unit may not suit all room aesthetics; needs outdoor space for condenser

Window-Type Aircon

Self-contained unit installed in a window or through a wall opening. The most affordable option with all components in o...

Cheapest, simplest installation, portable between rooms Noisiest type (40-55dB), blocks window, less energy-efficient than split

Cassette-Type Aircon

Recessed into the ceiling panel with only a grille visible. Distributes air in 4 directions for even cooling across larg...

Clean ceiling look, even cooling across large areas, space-saving Requires drop ceiling, more expensive installation, harder to service

Floor-Mounted / Floor-Standing Aircon

Indoor unit sits on or near the floor rather than high on a wall. Ideal for rooms with limited wall space, high ceilings...

Good for high ceilings, easy to service, strong airflow Takes up floor space, visible unit and piping

Ducted / Concealed Aircon

Indoor unit is hidden in the ceiling or a closet and connected to rooms via ducts. The ultimate invisible cooling — idea...

Invisible, quietest, serves multiple rooms, integrates with ventilation Highest installation cost, requires ceiling space, hardest to retrofit
